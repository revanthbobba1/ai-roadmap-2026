Key Insight #3

Prompting meaningfully shifts behavior

🧭
System Prompts
Developer-set instructions loaded before every user message. Like an instructions.md file for the model.
Adding "you are a customer support agent" changed response tone, scope, and escalation behavior — no retraining needed.
🔗
Chain-of-Thought
Prompt the model to reason step-by-step before giving an answer.
Claude showed implicit CoT even without explicit prompting. Explicit CoT forced visible reasoning in English prose.
🔒
Hard Constraints
Specifying exact output format, type, or length in the prompt.
GPT-4o was stricter — it refused floats when the prompt said "list of integers." Claude softened the constraint.
